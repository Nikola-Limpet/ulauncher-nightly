# Sleepy companions

The built-in imagegen tool generated the three original animals, then edited each
one to add bedding and sleepwear. The final assets are transparent 1536×1024 PNG files.
GTK composites them over the SVG sky at small logical sizes, leaving the search
field and results clear. There are no animation timers.

- `cat.png`: charcoal cat, lavender bed, quilt, and star-tipped nightcap.
- `panda.png`: panda in a navy bed, moon pillow, quilt, and sleeping cap.
- `fox.png`: curled fox, lavender bed, star quilt, and matching nightcap.

The assets retain their original resolution for HiDPI displays. Their alpha
channels were preserved. The external files shown as edit references below were
the earlier generated animal variants; the packaged PNG files are the final edits.

## Cat edit prompt

Edit this existing cat asset, preserving this exact charcoal sleeping cat, silver
face markings, lavender scarf, soft painted illustration style and three-quarter
pose. Add a cozy low oval padded pet bed beneath it, muted dusty lavender outer
bolster and deep navy cushion with a small quilted blanket tucked around
hindquarters. Add a small floppy indigo nightcap on its head, lavender rim and tiny
pale star at tip, keep ears and face visible. Whole cat and entire bed visible
with generous padding, compose as wide horizontal silhouette readable at 110px
wide. True transparent background, remove any external halo; no room, no floor,
no text or floating Z letters. It will sit on an indigo night-sky header.
